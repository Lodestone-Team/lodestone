Your Minecraft server, on your own machine.

Why pay for server hosting when you already have a powerful gaming PC? We certainly don't want to do that!
Lodestone Host is a free, open source server manager: start a server from a modpack, add mods from Modrinth, and share it with friends without port forwarding.

Download for Windows Open the dashboard

Installs and updates with Lodestone CLI · Docker · All downloads

  • Vanilla, Fabric, Forge and Paper servers, plus Fabric modpacks from Modrinth.
  • Play together without port forwarding: share a server with an invite link - that's it
  • Players get the right mods. Their Lodestone Launcher installs the mods your server needs and joins in one click.
  • Always private. Your servers and worlds stay on your machine, and we never see your data or sessions.
  • Users and permissions for everyone who helps run your servers.
  • API keys and a CLI for scripts, bots and agents.
