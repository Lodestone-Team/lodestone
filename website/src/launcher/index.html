Deceivingly simple, incredibly powerful.

A free, open source launcher for Minecraft. Modpacks, mods, shaders and resource packs from Modrinth, and your friends' servers, in one fast app.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 and 11 · All downloads

  • Fast: built on GPUI, the same technology as Discord's Project Phoenix.
  • Join a friend's server in one click. Open their invite link, or add them as a friend and skip it: the launcher sets up an instance with the server's mods and connects, without port forwarding.
  • Set up for your computer: new instances get memory, render distance and settings that suit your computer and their mods.
  • Your own servers too: start, stop and join Lodestone Host's servers from the launcher.
