Each file holds the unmodified path data of one icon, wrapped in an <svg> element:
arrow-up-right-from-square, caret-down, check, copy, expand, gear, plus,
right-from-bracket, square-plus, triangle-exclamation, user and xmark; circle-info,
circle-check and circle-exclamation; link, user-group, chevron-down, chevron-right,
chevron-left, pen, floppy-disk and circle-user.

window-minimize, window-maximize, window-restore and window-close are plain
lines and squares drawn for the launcher's own window buttons, which it shows on
Linux desktops that leave window decorations to apps. They are part of
Lodestone and under its license.
